The two optional GGUF adapters are modifications for Qwen3.5-4B; their training and target tensors are documented in the writer_adapter and laptop_adapter directories. The original model and quantization are not bundled. See the [upstream model](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B), [model license](Qwen-LICENSE.txt) and each experiment’s report. Bundled Liberation Sans fonts have their own license in resumeforge/fonts/LICENSE.txt.
